During Iran’s January protests, people recorded what they saw and heard as events unfolded around them.

This Is Our Story brings those moments together, preserving a record of the voices and images that emerged from those days.

A four-part documentary series, with English subtitles.

در جریان اعتراضات دی‌ماه، مردم آنچه را در خیابان‌ها می‌دیدند و می‌شنیدند، هم‌زمان با وقوع رویدادها ثبت کردند.
